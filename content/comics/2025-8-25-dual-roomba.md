[Cueball holds a finger up in front of White Hat. Behind Cueball, to the right, there is a stationary Roomba on the floor.]

Cueball: The Roomba keeps my floor clean.

Cueball: But what do I do when the ***Roomba*** gets dirty?

[In a frame-less panel White Hat looks over Cueball's shoulder as he has turned and gestures  towards two Roombas on the floor. The new Roomba is climbing up a ramp on to the first, still stationary, Roomba. Small lines behind the climbing Roomba indicates movement.]

Cueball: Simple:

Cueball: I got a second Roomba and a ramp, so they can clean each other.

[White Hat looks on as Cueball kneels down to flip the first Roomba over. He turns it on so it begins to move even though it is turned over, this is indicated with motion lines. The ramp is next to it, and the other Roomba is next to the ramp, now that one is stationary.]

Cueball: After the tops are clean, I flip one of them over so they can clean each others’ undersides.

[Cueball and White Hat are now watching the Roombas clean each other. The second Roomba has used the ramp behind them to climb on top of the first. Both of them are wiggling as indicated with several motion lines around them.]

White Hat: There’s nothing NSFW about this, and yet it’s the most NSFW thing I’ve every seen.

Cueball: Yeah, my YouTube DIY video about this got demonetized.
