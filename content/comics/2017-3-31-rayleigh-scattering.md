[Jill asks Miss Lenhart a question which she answers while lifting her arm towards Jill.]

Jill: Why is the sky blue?

Miss Lenhart: Because air is blue.

[Megan walks in from behind Jill.]

Megan: No, the sky is blue because of Rayleigh scattering–

Miss Lenhart: Nah, it's because air is blue. Blue light bounces off it and hits our eyes. Same as why anything is any color.

[Zoom in on Miss Lenhart's face.]

Miss Lenhart: It's why far-off mountains look blue – because of all the blue air in the way.

[Zoom out to Megan standing longer from Jill than Miss Lenhart who has thrown her arms out. Jill is facing directly out towards the reader.]

Megan: There's a specific quantum mechanism by which–

Miss Lenhart: Yeah but there's a physics mechanism for *every* color. You don't have to get all quantum right away.

[Frameless panel with Jill looking up at Miss Lenhart who stands holding her hands on her sides. Megan speaks from off-panel.]

Megan (off-panel): ...OK, I guess.

Miss Lenhart: Any other questions?

Jill: How do planes stay up?

[Miss Lenhart holds a finger up in front of Jill while Megan now is the one to throw out her arms.]

Megan: Well, the airflow–

Miss Lenhart: Tiny birds in the wings. Thousands. Flapping hard.

Jill: WOW!

Megan: *NO!*
