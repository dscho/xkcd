[Megan is sitting at her desk looking at a laptop. Cueball stands behind her.]

Megan: I'm browsing the list of potentially habitable planets.

Megan: This one is promising!

Megan: Wait, no, the surface is thousands of degrees.

[Similar panel.]

Megan: This one could be a water planet!

Megan: ... But it's not dense enough to be solid.

Cueball: Yeah, that's a mini-Neptune.

[Megan points at the laptop screen.]

Megan: This one could be habitable if it has a greenhouse effect!

Megan: Sadly, spectroscopy shows no atmosphere.

Cueball: Aww.

Megan: Here's one in the habitable zone, 1 Earth mass, with an atmosphere!

Cueball: Ooh, what star does it orbit?

Megan: ...Sol.

Cueball: *Dammit.*
