[Cueball and Megan are looking down at a baby Kidball, throwing its arms in the air, standing between them.]

Cueball: Oh man, we made a baby.

Megan: *Don't panic. Don't panic.*

Baby Kidball: Baby!

[Cueball looks at Megan, who still look down at the baby, which now looks down at her feet.]

Cueball: Parenting can't be that hard. Let's just do what comes naturally.

[In a frame-less panel The three of them stand there. Cueball and Megan look down and Kidball has spread his arms out. Beat frame.]

[A caption is in a frame at the top of the panel. Cueball and Megan are looking down between them. There are now two baby Kidballs. The larger one is looking at Cueball's feet while the smaller Kidball is looking at Megan's feet.]

Caption: Soon:

Megan: Aw, crap.
