[Beret Guy is spinning on a chair, holding two weights away from him. Hairbun and Cueball stand watching him.]

Beret Guy: See, when I hold the weights out, I rotate slowly.

[Beret Guy holds the weights closely, and spins faster. Cueball has his hand up as if thinking.]

Beret Guy: But if I pull them in, I spin faster, due to conservation of angular momentum.

[Beret Guy is spinning much faster, and his chair is moving upwards, tilting slightly to the left with beams blasting out. Hairbun and Cueball are backing away.]

Beret Guy: Now, to help shed momentum, I form an axial jet!
