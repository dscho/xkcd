[The first panel is split by a jagged diagonal to indicate a telephone conversation. Megan and Cueball are each on the phone in separate parts of the panel.]

Megan: I know you're not that into my sister, but she's really crushing on you.

Cueball: Yeah, it's awkward.

[Megan on the phone.]

Megan: She's in a rough spot. It's a lot to ask, but could you take her out and... dissuade her, without rejecting her?

[Cueball on the phone, standing.]

Cueball: Wait a second. Are you asking me to show her a mediocre time?

Megan [voice from phone]: I know it's a weird—

[Closeup on Cueball on the phone, raising his index finger.]

Cueball: No, no! This is the mission I was *born* for.

Megan [voice from phone]: I figured you could handle it.

Cueball: One of my classic high-school dates coming up!

Megan [voice from phone]: Oh God. Don't overdo it.
