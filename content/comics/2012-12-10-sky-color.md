Jill: Mommy, why is the sky blue?

Mother: Rayleigh scattering! Short wavelengths get scattered *way* more (proportional to 1/*<span title="lambda">λ</span>*<sup>4</sup>). Blue light dominates because it's so short.

Jill: Oh.

Jill: So why *isn't* the sky violet?
