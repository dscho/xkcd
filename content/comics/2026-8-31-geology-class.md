[Ponytail is teaching a class to Cueball and Megan. Ponytail is holding a pointer.]

Ponytail: Welcome to Geology 201.

Ponytail: Today we'll be review-bombing an Applebees.

[Ponytail points toward a map on the wall.]

Ponytail: Tomorrow we'll do Costco and Sephora.

Megan: Why?

Ponytail: If we cut traffic to the Northgate Shopping Center, it will go to the East Plaza instead.

[Zoom-in to Ponytail pointing to the map of the city, with several roads and intersections marked, and a river running through it.]

Ponytail: This will push the state to cancel the North Bridge project and shift funds to the Highway 3 extension...

[Back to a wide view of the class.]

Ponytail: ...creating new roadcuts through Triassic shale.

Megan: I ***like*** Applebees.

Ponytail: Then you should have told the city to approve my dig permit.
